## Formule addizione e sottrazione

$$
\sin(\alpha\pm \beta)=\sin \alpha \cos \beta \pm \cos \alpha \sin \beta \implies \sin(2\alpha)=2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha
$$
$$
\cos(\alpha\pm \beta)=\cos \alpha \cos \beta\pm \sin \alpha \sin \beta\implies \cos(2\alpha)=\cos^2\alpha-\sin^2\alpha
$$
